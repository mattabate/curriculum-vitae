\noindent\:\:\textbf{\large{Biographical Sketch}}
\vspace{.1cm}
\hrule
\vspace{.2cm}

\begin{tabular}{ m{7.2in}}
\setlength{\parskip}{.6em}%
I am an applied mathematician and researcher, living in Jersey City, New Jersey.

From 2017 to Fall 2022, I was a Ph.D. student and academic researcher at Georgia Tech. My research proposed new math-theoretic tools and algorithms studying systems of differential equations with disturbances.

I'm currently employed with TextQL Inc.,\ an New York based AI startup that I joined in 2024.
My primary role at TextQL is to manage our internal data systems and our internal deployment of AI agents.

Outside of work, I enjoy solving puzzles and studying sequences and patterns. I hope to one day find a solution to the Collatz Conjecture.
